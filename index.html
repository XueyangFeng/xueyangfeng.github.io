    💡 About Me

    Hi! I am Xueyang Feng (冯雪扬), a PhD student at the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence (GSAI) of Renmin University of China, supervised by Xu Chen. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Northwestern Polytechnical University in 2023.

    My research focuses on LLM Agents. My long-term goal is to build agents that understand user needs and reliably complete complex real-world tasks. I am particularly interested in:

    • Long-horizon agents: Web agents, skill discovery and composition, and dynamic multi-agent orchestration.
    • Personalized and interactive agents: conversational recommendation, user simulation, long-term memory, and emotional support.
    • Agent post-training: agentic reinforcement learning, preference optimization, and on-policy distillation.

    Feel free to contact me at xueyangfeng@ruc.edu.cn.
